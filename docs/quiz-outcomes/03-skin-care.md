# Skin care

Shown after someone picks "Skin care" on U1. 3 questions, 16 answer cards and 12 mixing rules. These questions are about the face.

## S1 · How does your skin usually feel by midday? (Choose one.)

### SK-S1-01 · Tight or dry
Evidence: Strong
- **What it may mean:** Extra moisture and a less drying cleansing step may be worth exploring.
- **Things to try:** 1. Gentle, fragrance-free cleanser. / 2. Cream rather than a thin lotion. / 3. Apply moisturizer while skin is still damp.
- **Ingredients that may help:** Glycerin: Helps attract water into skin. / Petrolatum: Helps reduce water leaving skin.
- **Watch out for:** Fragrance, retinol and glycolic acid, alcohol-based cleansers.
- **What the app says:** "Based on the tight or dry feeling you selected, gentle cleansing and a richer moisturizer may fit."
- **Every skin tone:** On deeper skin, dryness can look ashy or grayish.

### SK-S1-02 · Comfortable
Evidence: Moderate
- **What it may mean:** You may prefer maintaining what feels comfortable rather than adding a new target.
- **Things to try:** 1. Keep tolerated products. / 2. Use gentle cleansing. / 3. Explore moisturizer only as useful for comfort.
- **Ingredients that may help:** Optional glycerin: Helps attract water into skin. / No special ingredient target follows from this answer.
- **Watch out for:** Anything that reacted before; do not add retinol and glycolic acid ingredients merely because a routine feels comfortable.
- **What the app says:** "Because you selected comfortable, this path starts with what already feels good rather than extra steps."

### SK-S1-03 · Shiny in some areas
Evidence: Moderate
- **What it may mean:** Different areas may suit different amounts or textures of moisturizer.
- **Things to try:** 1. Gentle cleanser. / 2. Light lotion or gel on shiny areas. / 3. Richer moisturizer only on areas that feel dry.
- **Ingredients that may help:** Glycerin: Helps attract water into skin. / Dimethicone: Helps limit moisture leaving skin.
- **Watch out for:** Alcohol-based cleansers; fragrance if sensitivity is also selected.
- **What the app says:** "Because shine is limited to some areas, you may prefer adjusting moisturizer by area."

### SK-S1-04 · Shiny all over
Evidence: Moderate
- **What it may mean:** A lighter-feeling routine may suit your finish preference, without aiming to remove all surface oil.
- **Things to try:** 1. Gentle face wash. / 2. Light lotion or gel moisturizer. / 3. Explore oil-free or non-comedogenic formulas.
- **Ingredients that may help:** Glycerin: Helps attract water into skin. / Dimethicone: Helps limit moisture leaving skin.

## Mixing rules: when skin answers meet

### SK-R01 · Tight or dry + Very sensitive
- **What changes:** Keep the richer-moisture suggestion; make cleanser and moisturizer fragrance-free. Suppress default retinol or acid suggestions.
- **Which answer wins:** The sensitivity answer wins on tolerance safeguards; the midday-feel answer sets texture.
- **What the app says:** "Because you selected tightness and strong sensitivity, a gentle, fragrance-free routine with a richer moisturizer may fit."

### SK-R02 · Shiny in some areas + Moisture and dryness
- **What changes:** Offer lighter texture on shiny areas and richer texture only where dryness is felt.
- **Which answer wins:** The skin goal answer preserves the moisture goal; the midday-feel answer localizes it.
- **What the app says:** "Because you selected both shine in some areas and dryness, you may prefer different amounts of moisturizer in different areas."

### SK-R03 · Shiny all over + Moisture and dryness
- **What changes:** Do not remove moisturizer because of shine. Start with a light lotion or gel.
- **Which answer wins:** The skin goal answer wins against any "skip moisture" interpretation; the midday-feel answer sets weight.
- **What the app says:** "Because moisture matters to you even when skin looks shiny, a lighter moisturizer may fit."

### SK-R04 · Very sensitive + Fragrance preferences
- **What changes:** Ask whether the person wants scent information or has experienced a reaction; default product exploration to fragrance-free.
- **Which answer wins:** The sensitivity answer wins on the recommendation; the skin goal answer remains the explanation topic.
- **What the app says:** "Because you selected strong sensitivity, this fragrance guide starts with fragrance-free options while explaining your choices."

### SK-R05 · Not usually sensitive + Fragrance preferences
- **What changes:** Explain scented and fragrance-free choices neutrally; do not promise that fragrance will be tolerated.
- **Which answer wins:** The skin goal answer sets the topic; the sensitivity answer does not cancel testing guidance.
- **What the app says:** "Because fragrance interests you and sensitivity is uncommon for you, this path explores preferences without assuming every scent will feel comfortable."

### SK-R06 · Tight or dry + Cleansing
- **What changes:** Prioritize gentle cleansing and moisturizing afterward; do not choose a stronger wash to achieve a "clean" feeling.
- **Which answer wins:** The midday-feel answer changes the cleanser recommendation; the skin goal answer keeps cleansing as the topic.
- **What the app says:** "Because you selected tightness and cleansing, this path explores a gentler wash and moisture afterward."

### SK-R07 · Shiny all over + Very sensitive + Cleansing
- **What changes:** Suggest a gentle fragrance-free wash; foaming is optional, not a requirement. Exclude alcohol-based cleansers.
- **Which answer wins:** The sensitivity answer wins over stronger oil-removal suggestions.
- **What the app says:** "Because you selected shine and strong sensitivity, a gentle cleanser may fit better than trying to remove every trace of oil."

### SK-R08 · Very sensitive + Texture or finish
- **What changes:** Compare feel only among gentler, fragrance-free options; suppress exfoliation suggestions.
- **Which answer wins:** The sensitivity answer sets safeguards; the skin goal answer selects comparison format.
- **What the app says:** "Because sensitivity and product feel both matter to you, this path compares gentler textures rather than adding exfoliation."

### SK-R09 · It changes depending on the day + Keeping my routine simple
- **What changes:** Keep one tolerated baseline; adjust amount or placement before adding separate day-specific routines.
- **Which answer wins:** The skin goal answer limits steps; the midday-feel answer permits flexibility.
- **What the app says:** "Because your skin changes and simplicity matters, this path starts with one routine that leaves room for small adjustments."

### SK-R10 · I'm still figuring it out + Moisture and dryness
- **What changes:** Respect the stated moisture goal without assigning a dry-skin label; explore one comfortable moisturizer.
- **Which answer wins:** The skin goal answer wins on goal; the midday-feel answer blocks a skin-type conclusion.
- **What the app says:** "You can explore moisture without deciding what skin-type label fits."

### SK-R11 · Keeping my routine simple + Understanding products or ingredients
- **What changes:** Explain the products already in the short routine; do not add a product for every ingredient category explained.
- **Which answer wins:** Simplicity wins on step count; understanding wins on explanation.

### SK-R12 · Moisture and dryness + Texture or finish
- **What changes:** Separate moisture function from desired feel: compare lighter and richer formulas rather than saying only a heavy cream can moisturize.
