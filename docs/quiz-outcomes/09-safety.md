# Safety

Cuidance is a guide, not a doctor. These rules sit on top of every answer card and every combination rule. Nothing in the quiz can switch them off.

1. **Guidance, never a diagnosis.** Results say "may fit" and "based on what you selected." Never "you have" or "fix."
2. **"Not sure" is a good answer.** It leads to a gentle next step, never a dead end or a guess.
3. **Symptoms trigger a doctor note.** Picking "Very sensitive" alone doesn't. A rash that keeps coming back does.
4. **Emergencies stay separate.** A severe reaction gets its own line, shown on every result, apart from the regular tips.

## Emergency line (shown on every result, separate from the tips)

**What the app says:** "If a reaction includes trouble breathing or swelling of your lips, tongue, or throat, seek emergency medical help right away."

It never sits inside a tip or behind a "Show more" button.

## When to suggest a professional

Friendly notes, tied to symptoms, never to a quiz label. Each note shows only when someone describes the symptom or plans the service. The wording is exactly what the app says.

## Other safety lines (shown next to the tip they belong to)

| Area | Note | What the app says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skin care | New-product testing note | "Before using a new product widely, you may want to try it on a small area first. The American Academy of Dermatology suggests testing twice a day for seven to ten days. For a cleanser, follow its usual rinse-off directions. Stop if a reaction appears." |
| Hair care | Dye-specific safety note | "Before each dye use, follow the label's skin-test instructions, even if you have used that dye before. FDA advises testing two days before use, including for salon coloring. If a reaction appears, do not use the dye." |
| Hair care | Heated-smoothing safety note | "If a heated smoothing treatment causes eye irritation, coughing, wheezing, or other breathing symptoms, stop the exposure and seek medical advice. Trouble breathing calls for urgent medical help." |
| Body care | New-product testing note | "Before using an unfamiliar skin-care product widely, you may want to test it on a small area first. AAD suggests testing twice a day for seven to ten days. Follow the product's usual directions, including rinsing off a cleanser. Stop if a reaction appears." |
