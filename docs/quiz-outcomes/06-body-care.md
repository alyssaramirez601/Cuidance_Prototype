## B1 · What kind of body care are you exploring? (Select all that apply.)

### BD-B1-01 · Cleansing and moisture
Evidence: Strong

## Mixing rules: when body care answers meet

### BD-R01 · Cleansing and moisture + Fragrance-free
- **What changes:** Suggest a fragrance-free cleanser and moisturizer; do not replace fragrance-free with unscented.
- **Which answer wins:** The body care priority answer filters scent; the body care area answer keeps the function.
- **What the app says:** "Because cleansing, moisture, and fragrance-free care matter to you, this path applies that preference to both steps."

### BD-R02 · Deodorant or antiperspirant + Fragrance-free
- **What changes:** First distinguish odor control from sweat reduction, then explore fragrance-free options for the wanted function.
- **Which answer wins:** Function determines category; fragrance-free filters the options.
- **What the app says:** "Because fragrance-free underarm care interests you, this path first clarifies whether odor control, sweat reduction, or both matter."

### BD-R03 · Deodorant or antiperspirant + Comfortable to use
- **What changes:** Include previous reactions, dry-skin application directions, and scent preference; do not automatically select aluminum-free.
- **Which answer wins:** Comfort safeguards win over marketing claims.
- **What the app says:** "Because comfort matters in your underarm care, this path checks tolerance and directions alongside the product's function."

### BD-R04 · Fragrance + Fragrance-free
- **What changes:** Interpret this as possible fragrance education or avoidance, not a contradiction requiring a scented recommendation.
- **Which answer wins:** Fragrance-free wins for product exploration; fragrance remains the topic.
- **What the app says:** "Because you selected fragrance and fragrance-free care, this path explains scent labels without assuming you want added scent."

### BD-R05 · Fragrance-free + A specific scent
- **What changes:** Ask whether the preferences apply to different products; do not silently merge them into one recommendation.
- **Which answer wins:** Neither wins until clarified; display education rather than a product filter.
- **What the app says:** "You selected both fragrance-free care and a scent preference, so this path keeps them separate until their roles are clear."

### BD-R06 · Cleansing and moisture + Quick to absorb
- **What changes:** Compare lotion, cream, and ointment feel; do not promise a lotion will address reported significant dryness as well as a richer formula.
- **Which answer wins:** The body care priority answer sets desired feel; any explicit dryness report modifies the comparison.
- **What the app says:** "Because moisture and a light after-feel matter, this path compares textures and their tradeoffs."

### BD-R07 · Hands and feet + Comfortable to use

- **Which answer wins:** Comfort limits added treatments; the body care area answer sets areas.

### BD-R08 · Nail care + Simple to use
- **What changes:** Prioritize trimming, leaving cuticles intact, and moisturizing; coatings remain optional.
- **Which answer wins:** Simplicity limits complexity; nail care does not imply polish.
- **What the app says:** "Because you want simple nail care, this path starts with gentle handling and surrounding-skin care."

### BD-R09 · Compare body-care products + Products I already use
- **What changes:** Request actual labels or names before comparing; show function, scent, format, and directions side by side.
- **Which answer wins:** Existing products define the comparison once supplied.
- **What the app says:** "Because you want to compare current products, this path starts with their actual functions and labels."

### BD-R10 · Understand a body-care ingredient + Body-care ingredients

## Rules across areas: when a skin care answer changes body care advice

### X-BD-01 · Very sensitive + A specific scent
- **What changes:** Ask whether sensitivity applies to the intended body area and whether scent has caused reactions. Start skin-contact product exploration with fragrance-free options; keep scent education available.
- **Which answer wins:** Tolerance safeguards win over a scented-product suggestion, not over the person's right to explore scent.
- **What the app says:** "Because you selected sensitivity and a scent preference, this path separates scent exploration from what may feel comfortable on your skin."

### X-BD-02 · Very sensitive + Deodorant or antiperspirant
- **What changes:** Ask about underarm experience. Compare fragrance-free options and directions; do not select aluminum-free automatically.
- **Which answer wins:** Actual underarm reactions win over a general facial-sensitivity label.
- **What the app says:** "Because sensitivity may matter in your underarm care, this path checks that area's experience before comparing options."

### X-BD-03 · Sometimes sensitive + Comfortable to use
- **What changes:** Prioritize known tolerated formulas and one-at-a-time exploration; clarify the affected body area.
- **Which answer wins:** Area-specific tolerance history wins over marketing labels.
- **What the app says:** "Because comfort matters and sensitivity sometimes occurs, this path starts with your experience of the area you want to care for."

### X-BD-04 · Tight or dry + Cleansing and moisture
- **What changes:** Ask whether body skin also feels dry. If yes, suggest gentle cleansing and cream or ointment options; otherwise retain a preference-led comparison.
- **Which answer wins:** Confirmed body dryness wins over inferred facial-to-body dryness.
- **What the app says:** "If your body skin also feels dry, a gentler wash and richer moisturizer may fit."

### X-BD-05 · Tight or dry + Quick to absorb
- **What changes:** If dryness is also present on the body, explain that lighter feel and moisture retention can involve tradeoffs; consider richer texture only where wanted.
- **Which answer wins:** Comfort and actual body dryness inform options; quick after-feel remains a valid preference.
- **What the app says:** "If the area also feels dry, this path compares a light after-feel with richer moisture options."

### X-BD-06 · Very sensitive + Hands and feet
- **What changes:** Keep care gentle; do not add acid peels, strong exfoliants, or scented treatments by default.
- **Which answer wins:** Safety and comfort safeguards win over category-driven treatment assumptions.
- **What the app says:** "Because sensitivity is part of your choices, this path starts with gentle hand and foot care and checks how those areas feel."

### X-BD-07 · Fragrance preferences + Fragrance-free
- **What changes:** Preserve fragrance-free as the explicit body-product preference. Use the skin-care fragrance answer as an education topic only.
- **Which answer wins:** The body care priority answer's explicit preference wins over the ambiguous skin-care topic.
- **What the app says:** "Because you selected fragrance-free body care, this path follows that preference while explaining scent labels."

### X-BD-08 · Keeping my routine simple + Products I already use
- **What changes:** Explain current products before adding replacements or steps.
- **Which answer wins:** Existing-product review and simplicity govern delivery.
- **What the app says:** "Because simplicity and current products matter, this path starts with what you already use."

### X-BD-09 · Understanding products or ingredients + Body-care ingredients
- **What changes:** Merge explanations; distinguish moisturizer functions from deodorant and antiperspirant functions.
- **Which answer wins:** One shared educational module replaces duplicated explanations.
- **What the app says:** "Because ingredients interest you across both areas, this path explains their roles once and shows where they differ."

### X-BD-10 · I'm still figuring it out + No strong preference
- **What changes:** Do not assign a body-skin type or preferred scent; start with one selected function.
- **Which answer wins:** Topics they picked win over uncertainty; uncertainty blocks guessed preferences.
- **What the app says:** "You can explore one body-care function without choosing a skin label or scent preference."
