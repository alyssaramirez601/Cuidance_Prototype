# Hair care

Shown after someone picks "Hair care" on U1. 3 questions, 17 answer cards and 12 mixing rules.

## H1 · How would you describe your hair texture? (Choose one.)

### HR-H1-01 · Straight
Evidence: Moderate
- **What it may mean:** A conditioner focused on the ends may be a useful starting point, without assuming an oily scalp or fine strands.
- **Things to try:** 1. Wash according to scalp oil and dirt. / 2. Start conditioner at the ends. / 3. Let hair dry a little before gentle combing.
- **Ingredients that may help:** Cetrimonium chloride: Helps reduce static and tangling. / Dimethicone: Helps lubricate strands during handling.
- **Watch out for:** Fragrance and anything that reacted before, treating "sulfate-free" as proof a shampoo is gentle; no texture-specific blacklist.
- **What the app says:** "Based on the straight texture you selected, conditioning the ends may be a comfortable starting point."

### HR-H1-02 · Wavy
Evidence: Moderate
- **What it may mean:** Conditioner amount and optional hold may be more useful to explore than a fixed wash schedule.
- **Things to try:** 1. Wash according to scalp oil and dirt. / 2. Explore rinse-out conditioner. / 3. Add a light styling product only if hold is wanted.
- **Ingredients that may help:** Cetrimonium chloride: Helps reduce static and tangling. / Styling film-formers: Help hold your chosen shape.
- **Watch out for:** Fragrance and anything that reacted before, treating "sulfate-free" as proof a shampoo is gentle, confusing conditioning alcohols with drying ones; no evidence-based rule that wavy hair should avoid silicones.
- **What the app says:** "Based on the wavy texture you selected, you may want to explore conditioning and optional hold separately."

### HR-H1-03 · Curly
Evidence: Moderate
- **What it may mean:** Curly hair, especially when thick, can be more prone to dryness and tangling, but this answer does not establish either for you.
- **Things to try:** 1. Condition the lengths after washing. / 2. Detangle gently with conditioner present. / 3. Explore leave-in conditioner if useful.
- **Ingredients that may help:** Behentrimonium chloride: Helps reduce friction during detangling. / Dimethicone: Helps lubricate strands during handling.
- **Watch out for:** Fragrance and anything that reacted before, treating "sulfate-free" as proof a shampoo is gentle; do not exclude conditioning silicones automatically.
- **What the app says:** "Based on the curly texture you selected, conditioning and gentle detangling may fit your wash-day care."

### HR-H1-04 · Coily
Evidence: Moderate
- **What it may mean:** Coily hair may benefit from careful lubrication and detangling, without assuming strand thickness, race, or a specific routine.
- **Things to try:** 1. Condition the lengths. / 2. Detangle gently in sections with conditioner. / 3. Explore leave-in conditioner if it improves handling.
- **Ingredients that may help:** Behentrimonium chloride: Helps reduce friction during detangling. / Dimethicone: Helps lubricate strands during handling.
- **Watch out for:** Fragrance and anything that reacted before, treating "sulfate-free" as proof a shampoo is gentle; oils are optional, not a substitute for scalp cleansing.
- **What the app says:** "Based on the coily texture you selected, this path gives extra attention to conditioning and gentle handling."

### HR-H1-05 · A mix of textures
Evidence: Moderate
- **What it may mean:** This answer supports flexible handling rather than assigning one texture to every section.
- **Things to try:** 1. Use one scalp-cleansing approach. / 2. Adjust conditioner amount by section. / 3. Detangle sections gently using the method that feels comfortable.
- **Ingredients that may help:** Cetrimonium chloride: Helps reduce static and tangling. / Dimethicone: Helps lubricate strands during handling.
- **Watch out for:** Fragrance and anything that reacted before, treating "sulfate-free" as proof a shampoo is gentle; do not assign separate ingredient lists to each section automatically.
- **What the app says:** "Because you selected a mix of textures, you may prefer adjusting care by section rather than choosing one label."

## Mixing rules: when hair answers meet

### HR-R01 · Coily + Heat-styled
- **What changes:** Keep conditioner-assisted detangling; add limited heat, low or medium settings, and a heat-protectant formula. Do not increase temperature because of the selected texture.
- **Which answer wins:** The styling answer's heat safeguards win over any texture-based temperature suggestion.
- **What the app says:** "Because you selected coily texture and heat styling, this path combines gentle detangling with lower heat exposure."

### HR-R02 · Curly + Frizz and manageability
- **What changes:** Prioritize conditioner, gentle sectioned detangling, and avoiding dry brushing. Leave-in is optional; no straightening assumption.
- **Which answer wins:** The hair texture answer changes handling; the hair goal answer sets the desired outcome.
- **What the app says:** "Because you selected curly texture and easier manageability, conditioning and gentler detangling may fit."

### HR-R03 · Straight + Moisture and dryness
- **What changes:** Start conditioning dry-feeling ends; do not equate straight texture with oily hair or omit conditioner.
- **Which answer wins:** The hair goal answer's dryness overrides an assumed low-conditioning preference.
- **What the app says:** "Because you selected straight texture and dryness, conditioning the ends may be a useful starting point."

### HR-R04 · Color-treated + Heat-styled
- **What changes:** Prioritize gentler handling and less heat; ask whether the color involved bleaching or permanent dye. No formula can make unlimited heat harmless.
- **Which answer wins:** Treatment-history and heat safeguards win over texture or finish preferences.
- **What the app says:** "Because your routine includes color and heat, this path gives extra attention to conditioning and limiting further exposure."

### HR-R05 · Protective styles + Cleansing and wash-day care
- **What changes:** Keep scalp cleansing in the routine; suggest a style that permits washing and rinsing. If access is difficult, discuss an adapted approach with a stylist.
- **Which answer wins:** Scalp access wins over a "leave it untouched" interpretation of protective styling.
- **What the app says:** "Because wash-day care matters while your hair is styled, this path focuses on scalp access and gentle cleansing."

### HR-R06 · Protective styles + Heat-styled
- **What changes:** Apply heat safeguards where heat is used and tension safeguards where hair is pulled; do not cancel either.
- **Which answer wins:** Both the styling answer's safeguards remain; neither replaces the other.
- **What the app says:** "Because your styling includes heat and protective styles, this path keeps both heat exposure and pulling in view."

### HR-R07 · Curly or Coily + Definition + Natural texture
- **What changes:** Offer optional gel or mousse after conditioning; keep natural variation acceptable and avoid promising uniform patterns.
- **Which answer wins:** The hair goal answer sets optional hold; the hair texture answer sets handling; the styling answer blocks assumed straightening.
- **What the app says:** "Because you want definition while wearing your natural texture, you may want to explore optional hold after conditioning."

### HR-R08 · Wavy + Definition
- **What changes:** Start with an optional small amount of hold product; compare feel before adding richer layers.
- **Which answer wins:** The hair goal answer sets the goal; the hair texture answer supplies a tentative starting approach, not a restriction.
- **What the app says:** "Because definition is your goal, you may want to start with a little hold and explore how it feels."

### HR-R09 · Moisture and dryness + Cleansing and wash-day care
- **What changes:** Separate scalp cleansing from conditioning the lengths; do not reduce washing automatically to address dry-feeling hair.
- **Which answer wins:** Scalp condition sets wash timing; dryness sets length care.
- **What the app says:** "Because you selected washing and dryness, this path pairs scalp cleansing with conditioning your lengths."

### HR-R10 · A mix of textures + Heat or color-treated care
- **What changes:** Adapt handling by section, but do not assume that every visibly different section has a different damage level. Ask about treatment location if relevant.
- **Which answer wins:** Known treatment history wins over texture-based assumptions.
- **What the app says:** "Because you selected mixed textures and treatment care, this path follows the sections and treatments you describe."

### HR-R11 · I'm not sure yet + Color-treated
- **What changes:** Keep the goal open, but include basic gentle handling and dye safety when recoloring is discussed.
- **Which answer wins:** The styling answer's safety stays even when the hair goal answer is uncertain.
- **What the app says:** "You can keep exploring your goal while this path accounts for the color treatment you selected."

### HR-R12 · I change it up + Styling
- **What changes:** Keep one cleansing-and-conditioning baseline; display method-specific guidance only for the methods actually selected or described.
- **Which answer wins:** Explicit heat, protective-style, or color selections win over the vague switching answer.
- **What the app says:** "Because you change styles, this path follows your chosen method without building a separate routine for every look."
